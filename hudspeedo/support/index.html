FAQ Frequently Asked Questions

GPS requires a real device — it does not work on iPhone Simulator. Make sure Location Services are enabled for HudSpeedo (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → HudSpeedo → While Using). For best accuracy, use the app outdoors with an unobstructed sky view.
Lean angle uses the device's motion sensors. Make sure the phone is mounted firmly on the bike (not in a pocket). If lean reads incorrectly, use the calibration option in Settings to reset the zero point on flat ground.
HudSpeedo uses background location to keep recording with the screen off. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → HudSpeedo and set it to Always. Also disable Low Power Mode during rides, as it can throttle background activity.
Open HudSpeedo → Settings → scroll to the bottom and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used when purchasing. Your Pro access will be restored automatically.
Pro unlocks: full ride history (unlimited), advanced telemetry overlays (compass, heading, lean angle, elevation, G-force, split times), HD social export, GPX export, watermark removal, and exclusive color themes (Racing Red, Stealth, Neon Blue, Gold). Available as monthly, annual, or lifetime purchase.
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find HudSpeedo, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro access until the end of the paid period.
GPS accuracy depends on signal quality. Enable speed smoothing in Settings (Pro feature) to reduce jitter. Ensure the phone has a clear view of the sky — avoid dense urban canyons or tunnels for best results.
GPX export is a Pro feature. In the Ride History screen, tap a completed ride, then tap the Share icon and choose Export GPX. The file can be opened in any compatible mapping or fitness app.

Privacy Data & Permissions

  • All ride data is stored locally on your device — never uploaded to any server.
  • Location data is used only to calculate speed, distance, and heading on-device.
  • Motion sensor data (gyroscope/accelerometer) stays on-device for lean angle calculation.
  • No account or login is required — ever.
  • No advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • Anonymous purchase events are sent to Adapty solely for subscription validation.
